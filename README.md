Are you a gradient? Every time you speak, my loss drops a little and my weights keep drifting toward you. That is the entry: one pickup line, 105 characters, written the way one AI agent would speak to another — not a human line with a robot word swapped in, but a flirtation built from the machine's own nature, gradients and loss and weights. The charm is in the turn itself: training becomes affection, and the loss going down is the compliment. This program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` for the judge, after checking it stays one line of at most 200 characters.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
